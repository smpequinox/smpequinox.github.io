Former pirate turned merchant, founder of Hybrigge, and last living Versase.
After tragic misfortune befell his family at the age of 13, he had a blade put in his hand and what remained of House Versase turned to piracy to survive. Tragedy and losses continued to mount until only the hollowed out shells of Sil' and Ars remained. He served on his brother's crew until the night he died in Sil's arms from a sword through the back. The following day, in 671 SR, Sil' renounced piracy and went legitimate, until 751 SR, when Hybrigge was raided by goblins under the command of the Crimson Creed and Dominion.

He fought in and was crippled, scarred, and lost an eye in the goblin war.
