## New player checklist

1. Read the [Rules]({{ '/rules/' | relative_url }}).
2. Join the [Discord]({{ site.discord_url }}).
3. Skim the [World Lore]({{ '/world-lore/' | relative_url }}) and the current arc.
4. Create a character concept with the [Character Creation]({{ '/character-creation/' | relative_url }}) guide.
5. Find a faction, faith, job, or starting location.
6. Submit your [application]({{ site.application_url }}).
7. Introduce yourself and find your first scene.

## Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **IC** | In-character. What your character knows and does in the story. |
| **OOC** | Out-of-character. Anything from outside the story, like Discord chat or streams. |
| **Metagaming** | Acting on information your character would not realistically know. |
| **Powergaming** | Forcing an outcome onto another character without letting them respond. |
| **Godmodding** | Ignoring damage or consequences, or deciding another player’s outcome. |
| **Kill Ticket** | A staff request required before any character kill. |
| **Heat of the Moment** | A narrow exception allowing immediate lethal action after a severe personal offense. |
| **Telepathica** | The in-character mind communication channel, with its own rules. |
| **Retcon** | A staff decision to reverse an event so it no longer counts in the story. |
