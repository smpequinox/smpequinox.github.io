After being discarded by his family for his bastard origins, Eychon seized the throne from the main bloodline after a long campaign of blood and death. His mercenary corps, the Black Banners, were known throughout the realm for their efficiency and were the pinnacle of Ashlander power in the modern era. Eychon himself stepped down from the throne after a failed assassination attempt, and has separated himself from the tags of his bloodline. Its curse still follows him.
