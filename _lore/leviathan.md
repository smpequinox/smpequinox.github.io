**[Content Warnings]**
> Themes of: _Cannibalism, Skin-walking, and Body Horror_

???, also known as Leviathan, is an artificially created monster known to have an insatiable hunger for anyone and anything. It is a face-stealer consisting of corrupted mana and there is very little information about how it was created in the first place. 

> "Morality _always_ gets in the way of things. I just wanna sit and eat whatever, is that so much to ask?"

Leviathan consists of a black mass/sludge that moves similarly to tar and smells closely related to rot. It takes energy from its hosts to keep itself alive and gives them extreme hunger pains that is nearly impossible to handle. Normal food does not quell it, only flesh from another being is what typically fills it for a time until that starvation inevitably returns. 

It is well known to multiply in some form of mitosis or hermaphroditism. Every part can be cut or separated which then duplicates from its parent body, of which every split has direct access to the central hivemind Leviathan has. (Meaning it can communicate between its bodies, allowing information to travel from one part to the other.)

> "Feed me now. Hungry... hungry... you might just starve like this... you'll kill us both..." 

**If any "Bottles of Leviathan" are found, or if a player has some form of encounter/sludge of it then immediately reach out to Staff for information or help.**
