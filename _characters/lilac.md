Lilac is the youngest of the Runework constructs created by House Cereus in order to protect, maintain, and raise the noble family's generations. She was created by Edward de Cereus's great-grandfather. After his death, she became the house's Gardener, for she had a fondness for flowers. Though she seemed almost stoic in the past, unable to show empathy or emotional responses to any given situation, instead she tended to be quiet, polite, frozen in the past. Though times have changed her, as new experiences have taught her to learn and grow, the hollow void still haunts her in the back of her mind.
