Growing up impoverished, Elderbach Gloveinschpire wanted nothing more than to be blessed with the gift of magic. This passion led to adulthood where he conned his way amongst some of the great mages of the land under the premise of "his unparalleled mana concealment". He was the apprentice of Gregorius Explidicia IV, where each lesson taught him more about the qualities of a wizard. Even though he has yet to learn any true magic, his previous experiences have undeniably led to a convincing front. He is eager to pursue any and all forms of magic/mana manipulation which has led to a great adaptation with runes. Throughout everything, that stagnant, undying passion to wield mana from his own being has yet to go away.
