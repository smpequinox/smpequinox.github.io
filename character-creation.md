## The path to your first scene

1. Read the [Rules]({{ '/rules/' | relative_url }}).
2. Choose a starting region, nation, faction, or faith from the [World Lore]({{ '/world-lore/' | relative_url }}).
3. Create a simple concept with one clear desire.
4. Add a flaw, a complication, and a relationship.
5. Check that your concept fits the public canon.
6. Submit your [application]({{ site.application_url }}).
7. Once approved, your character can be added to the [Character Gallery]({{ '/character-gallery/' | relative_url }}).

## Character sheet

Copy this into your application or notes and fill in each line.

| Field | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Full name |
| **Pronouns** | Your character’s pronouns |
| **Age or life stage** | Age, or the setting’s equivalent |
| **Species or origin** | If relevant to the setting |
| **Home** | Nation, region, or settlement |
| **Occupation** | What they do day to day |
| **Affiliations** | Factions, religions, mentors |
| **Appearance** | Clear visual details others can picture |
| **Personality** | Three traits and one contradiction |
| **Core desire** | What they want |
| **Pressure** | What makes it difficult |
| **Fear or flaw** | What can complicate scenes |
| **Secret** | Optional. Spoiler-sensitive secrets go to staff, not the public wiki |
| **Relationships** | At least one connection |
| **Player boundaries** | Topics or outcomes that need consent |
| **Story hooks** | Three ways others can meet or involve them |

## Canon and balance checklist

Before you submit, make sure your character:

- Fits the public setting and the current era
- Has strengths **and** meaningful limitations
- Does not control another player’s character
- Leaves room for growth and consequences
- Uses secrets to create play, not to bypass it

> Major abilities, titles, or supernatural traits need staff approval. See rule 2.5 in the [Rules]({{ '/rules/' | relative_url }}).
