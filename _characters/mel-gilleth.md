born as Melinda Gilleth, Mel was born into a noble family that has deep history about ancient magic and illusion spells. She was sheltered at a young age, loved by her older siblings and was forced to go through intense lessons from the members of her people. One day, the Gilleth Manor was ambushed by mysterious groups of assassin, leading Mel as the sole survivor of the Gilleth Massacure. She undergo into hiding for over 10 years, altering her appearance and her identity.

despite the losses of her closes ones, she begin to accept her real identity and accepts the challenges she faced along the way and never run away from fear. Now, she is currently rebuilding House Gilleth to a new era, with her adopted daughters, Jasmine and Mani. Who knows what lies ahead of this kind yet poor soul.
