> All general rules are enforceable. Breaking them may result in warnings, temporary bans, or permanent bans at staff discretion.

## 1. General server rules

### 1.1 Cheating and exploits

Hacked or modified clients are prohibited. This includes:

- X-ray
- Kill aura
- Fly
- Reach
- Speed
- Auto-clickers
- Duping tools
- Automation software

Duplication glitches are forbidden unless explicitly allowed by staff. Exploiting server bugs or unintended mechanics is prohibited. Any action that gives an unfair advantage over other players is not allowed.

### 1.2 Harassment and hate speech

Slurs, bullying, personal attacks, real-life threats, and doxxing are forbidden. Sustained out-of-character harassment is prohibited. In-character hostility does not excuse out-of-character abuse.

### 1.3 Staff authority

- Staff rulings are final.
- Impersonating staff members is punishable.
- Public arguments about staff decisions must stop when staff ask.
- Appeals must be submitted privately and respectfully.
- Staff may retcon any in-character event, death, or occurrence to maintain fairness.

### 1.4 Server integrity and performance

Lag-inducing builds, machines, or redstone contraptions may be removed. Malicious or intentional lag abuse may result in immediate punishment. Staff may request changes to builds that cause sustained server lag.

### 1.5 Content standards

NSFW builds, skins, usernames, or messages are not allowed. Extreme graphic content and sexual content are forbidden. All content must remain appropriate to the server theme.

### 1.6 Griefing and unauthorized destruction

Random griefing, theft, or destruction outside of roleplay systems is prohibited. All damage to builds or property must follow roleplay and server rules.

### 1.7 Ban evasion

Using alternate accounts to bypass punishments will result in the permanent removal of all associated accounts.

## 2. Roleplay rules

### 2.1 IC vs OOC

**IC (in-character)** is information your character learns through gameplay and roleplay. **OOC (out-of-character)** is knowledge gained from Discord, streams, spectators, or other outside sources. OOC knowledge may not influence IC actions.

### 2.2 Metagaming

Acting on information your character would not realistically know is prohibited. For example:

- Using OOC chat to influence IC decisions
- Tracking players using mechanics your character would not understand
- Knowing secret plans without discovering them through roleplay

### 2.3 Powergaming

Forcing outcomes or actions onto another character is prohibited. Every action must give the other player a chance to respond.

| Not allowed | Allowed |
| --- | --- |
| “I stab you and you die.” | “I attempt to stab toward your chest.” |

### 2.4 Godmodding

Characters may not ignore damage or consequences. Characters must operate within the server lore and mechanics.

### 2.5 Roleplay integrity

- Characters must remain consistent and believable.
- Overpowered characters require staff approval.
- Major abilities, titles, or supernatural traits must be approved by staff.
- Players may not create lore that directly affects other characters without permission.

### 2.6 OOC conflict

In-character hostility does not justify out-of-character harassment. Personal insults or attacks outside of roleplay are prohibited.

## 3. Combat and kill rules

### 3.1 Kill tickets

All character kills require a Kill Ticket. A Kill Ticket must include:

- The target character
- The in-character reason for the kill
- A summary of the events leading up to the kill
- Relevant evidence or context

Staff may approve the request, deny it, ask for more context, or apply conditions.

## 4. PvP rules

Player-versus-player combat is restricted and regulated to keep roleplay fair and prevent abuse. Unauthorized PvP may result in punishment, or the encounter may be retconned.

### 4.1 Combat approval

All combat between players must be approved through a ticket. PvP outside an approved ticket is not allowed unless every participating party explicitly consents. If any participant does not consent, combat may not happen without staff approval through the ticket system.

### 4.2 Combat restrictions

During PvP combat, the following apply:

| Not allowed | Allowed placeable blocks |
| --- | --- |
| Water buckets | Buttons |
| Lava buckets | Pressure plates |
| Placing blocks | Levers |

Allowed blocks may only be used to interact with mechanisms or the environment. Trying to get around these restrictions through indirect placement or similar mechanics is prohibited.

### 4.3 Heat of the Moment clause

In rare situations, immediate lethal action may happen without prior ticket approval. This applies when a character encounters someone responsible for a severe personal offense, such as:

- The murderer of a close family member
- The kidnapper of a loved one
- Other severe personal crimes directly tied to the character

The encounter must happen within a reasonable time after the event and must be believable in the roleplay. The clause does not apply to events far in the past, or to situations with significant time for premeditation. It covers an act of rage and a split-second decision, not a methodically planned attack.

### 4.4 Staff review of Heat of the Moment actions

Using the Heat of the Moment clause carries the highest chance of staff review and possible retcon. These events may be reversed for even minor reasons if the situation is found unfair to either party. The clause is an exception to the ticket system and will be examined carefully.

### 4.5 Two-sided standard

The Heat of the Moment clause applies to both sides: the attacker and the defender are examined equally.

> If a character murders someone’s grandmother and is then found by the victim’s grandson shortly afterward, the confrontation may reasonably escalate. If the murderer chooses to stand in a public or exposed place right after the act, staff will weigh that too.

Actions on both sides are evaluated to decide whether the encounter was reasonable within the roleplay.

### 4.6 Retcon enforcement

Lore deaths found to break these rules may be completely retconned. If a death is invalidated, the events involved may be reversed and any outcomes tied to that death may be nullified. Staff keep final authority to reverse encounters that break PvP rules or undermine fair roleplay.

## 5. Telepathica rules

These rules apply whenever you use the Telepathica (mind communication). Keep it immersive, clean, and easy to follow.

### 5.1 OOC usage

- All OOC must be written in (parentheses), and kept to one or two short sentences.
- Longer clarifications or discussions can go in a thread attached to the message.
- Do not use `//`, brackets, or any other OOC format. If it is in parentheses, it is OOC. Everything else is IC.

### 5.2 Directed thoughts

- **Pinging a player** (@) means your character is deliberately directing a thought at them. The Telepathica alerts the recipient, but a ping does not force their character to respond, notice right away, or acknowledge it.
- **Messages without a ping** are general broadcasts, visible to anyone viewing or taking part in the Telepathica.
- **Mass pings** should be used sparingly and only when they make sense in character. Excessive or unnecessary mass pings may be treated as spam.

### 5.3 Not allowed

- **GIFs** are not permitted.
- **Media must be in character.** Images, screenshots, and videos must fit the setting. Modern, meme, or immersion-breaking media is not allowed.
- **Videos must be uploaded as files.** Links to YouTube, TikTok, and similar sites are not permitted.
- **No meta knowledge.** Do not send information your character would not reasonably know.
- **No spamming or flooding** others with excessive or rapid messages.
- **No modern or out-of-setting content**, including memes, modern references, or real-world content unless server lore supports it.
- **No rule bypassing.** Do not hide OOC information, sneak in meta knowledge, or abuse formatting to get around these rules.

### 5.4 Expectations

- Keep communication mostly in character, and use OOC only when necessary.
- Keep messages clear and readable.
- Make sure all shared media is immersive and fits the setting.
- Respect the Telepathica’s purpose as an in-character communication space.

### 5.5 Enforcement

Breaking these rules may result in message removal or voiding, warnings, roleplay penalties, or further moderation for repeated violations. The Telepathica exists to enrich the world, not break immersion.

## Roleplay standards

- **Stay in character.** Let scenes breathe and avoid derailing serious moments.
- **No godmodding.** Do not decide another player’s actions, injuries, emotions, or outcomes.
- **No metagaming.** Do not use information your character has not learned.
- **Honor consequences.** Character choices should create story, not erase it.
- **Share the spotlight.** Make room for quieter characters and new players.

## Player agreement

By playing on Equinox SMP, you agree to follow the rules above, respect staff decisions while an issue is reviewed, and help keep the server welcoming.
