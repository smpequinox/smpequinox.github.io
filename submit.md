Players can add to the wiki through submission forms. Staff review every submission before it goes live.

<p><a class="button" href="{{ site.submit_url }}">Open the submission forms</a></p>

## How it works

1. **Sign in to GitHub.** The forms live on GitHub, where this wiki is stored. A free account is all you need: [create one here](https://github.com/signup).
2. **Choose what you're submitting.** Pick a type from the list, then fill in its form.
3. **Staff review it.** They may ask questions or suggest changes in the comments on your submission.
4. **It goes live.** Once approved, your submission becomes a wiki page and appears in the right section automatically.

**Updating your page later?** Use the same form and paste your page's address in "Updating an existing page?" at the top. Only fill in what's changed: empty boxes keep what's already there, and new bonds are added to your existing ones. To remove something, write a single `-` in its box; to remove a bond, write `Name | remove`.

## Before you submit

- **Everything is public.** Anyone can read submissions, even before they're approved. Never include secrets, spoilers or private information.
- **Characters must be approved first.** Only submit a character once staff have accepted their application.
- **Credit art properly.** Only attach art you made or have permission to use, and say who drew it.
- **Lore must be canon.** Submit lore only for things that have happened or been revealed in play. Ideas and guesses belong in a theory.
